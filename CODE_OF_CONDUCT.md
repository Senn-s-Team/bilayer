# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that builds this project up:

- Being kind and patient with people who are learning.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Crediting other people's work.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberately inflammatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when someone
is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and issues that do not comply. They will
communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and
security of the reporter will be respected.
